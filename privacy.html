Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026 • Effective Date: September 1, 2026

ThaiFluent ("we," "our," or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and website (thaifluent.com) handle your data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

ThaiFluent is architected as an offline-first application. We do NOT require you to create an account, provide an email address, or enter personal information to use the learning features.

2. Local Storage & AES-256 Encrypted Backups

All your learning statistics, XP, streaks, spaced repetition (SRS) card reviews, and bookmarks are stored exclusively on your device. If you use the Export Backup feature, your data is locally encrypted using AES-256 before saving to your device file storage.

3. In-App Purchases

If you purchase ThaiFluent Pro, transactions are processed directly by the Google Play Store (or Apple App Store). We do not collect, view, or store your credit card numbers or financial details.

4. No Third-Party Tracking or Ads

The app contains zero third-party advertisements and zero advertising trackers (No Google AdMob, No Facebook SDK, No Advertising ID tracking).

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is safe for language learners of all ages.
