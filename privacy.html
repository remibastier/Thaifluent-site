Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 1, 2026 • Effective Date: September 1, 2026

At ThaiFluent (available at thaifluent.com and on the Google Play Store as com.thaifluent.app), we believe that learning a language should be a private, focused, and distraction-free experience. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to zero data collection and total transparency.

1. Local-First Storage Philosophy

ThaiFluent is built from the ground up as an offline-first mobile application. All of your learning data, including:

  • Lesson scores, test histories, and unlocked module progress
  • Spaced Repetition (SRS) memory review intervals
  • Experience points (XP), daily study streaks, and earned milestones
  • Vocabulary bookmarks and user preferences (voice pitch, reminder times)

are stored exclusively on your local device using secure local databases and encrypted key-value storage. We do not transmit or store your learning data on external cloud servers.

2. Audio & Speech Playback Permissions

ThaiFluent utilizes your mobile device's native Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine (such as Google Speech Services on Android) and local studio audio assets for audio playback.

  • No Voice Recording: The app does not secretly record, monitor, or upload your voice to any remote server.
  • Local Processing: Speech synthesis parameters (such as pitch modulation for male and female voices) are executed entirely on your hardware.

3. Zero Third-Party Tracking & Advertising

We respect your attention and digital privacy:

  • No Ad Networks: ThaiFluent does not include third-party advertising SDKs or data brokers.
  • No Location Tracking: We do not access, track, or share your GPS or geographical location.
  • No Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

4. Local Encrypted Backups & Data Portability

ThaiFluent allows you to export your full learning history into an encrypted .thaifluent file. You have complete control over where this file is saved (e.g. your local storage, personal Google Drive, or shared via your own email). You may choose to protect your backup with a personal AES-256 password.

5. Data Deletion & Reset

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you have full authority to delete it at any time:

  • You can tap "Reset Progress" in the app's Settings tab to instantly purge all stored learning records.
  • Uninstalling the application from your device permanently removes all local app data.
